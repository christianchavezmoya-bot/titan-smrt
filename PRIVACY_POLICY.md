# Titan SMRT - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 1, 2024**

## Introduction

Titan SMRT ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our fitness tracking application.

By downloading and using Titan SMRT, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use our application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email Address**: Used for account creation and authentication
- **Username**: Your chosen display name
- **Password**: Securely hashed and stored (never stored in plain text)
- **Profile Data**: Optional information including:
  - Age
  - Gender
  - Height
  - Weight
  - Fitness goals (e.g., strength, hypertrophy, fat loss)
  - Activity level

### 2. Fitness Data

We collect data related to your fitness activities:

- **Workout Logs**: Exercise name, sets, reps, weight, RPE, rest times
- **Exercise History**: All exercises you've performed
- **Routines**: Custom workout templates you create
- **Nutrition Data**: Daily macro tracking (protein, carbs, fats)
- **Performance Metrics**: Calculated 1RM, volume, PR records
- **Progress Data**: Historical workout and performance trends

### 3. Health Data (Optional)

With your explicit consent, we may collect:

- **Heart Rate Data**: From Apple Watch, Garmin, or other BLE devices
- **Calorie Data**: Estimated calories burned during workouts
- **Sleep Data**: If available and relevant to recovery analysis
- **Step Count**: Daily activity levels

### 4. Media Data

- **Exercise Videos**: Form analysis videos you upload for AI coaching
- **Profile Photos**: Optional profile pictures you choose to upload
- **Screenshots**: In-app screenshots for support purposes (only with your consent)

### 5. Device and Technical Data

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Usage Data**: Features used, session duration, crash reports
- **Location Data**: Only if you explicitly enable location features (not currently used)

### 6. Subscription and Payment Data

- **Subscription Status**: Free or Pro tier
- **Payment Information**: Processed through secure third-party payment providers (Apple App Store, Google Play)
- **Transaction History**: Purchase dates and amounts (we do not store full payment card details)

## How We Collect Your Information

### Direct Collection

You provide information directly when you:

- Create an account or profile
- Log workouts and exercises
- Track nutrition and macros

### Automatic Collection

We automatically collect:

- **Usage Analytics**: How you use the app features
- **Performance Data**: App performance metrics

### Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Firebase Analytics**: For anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting
- **Apple App Store / Google Play**: For subscription management and payments
- **PostgreSQL**: For secure cloud storage of your data

## Data Storage and Security

### 1. Local Storage

- **Primary Storage**: SQLite database on your device
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted at rest on your device
- **Access**: Only the Titan SMRT app can access this data

### 2. Cloud Storage

- **Primary Storage**: Secure PostgreSQL database
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL
- **Access**: You control access through your account credentials
- **Backup**: Regular automated backups for data protection

### 3. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

- **Authentication**: Secure password hashing with bcrypt
- **Session Management**: JWT tokens with expiration
- **Data Encryption**: AES-256 encryption for sensitive data
- **Secure Communication**: HTTPS/TLS for all data transmission
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access for our systems
- **Regular Audits**: Security reviews and penetration testing

## Your Rights and Choices

### 1. Access and Control

You have the right to:

- **Access Your Data**: View, edit, or delete your personal information
- **Export Your Data**: Download all your data in a portable format
- **Delete Your Account**: Permanently remove your account and all associated data
- **Opt Out of Analytics**: Disable anonymous usage tracking in settings
- **Control Sharing**: Choose what data to share publicly

### 2. Data Portability

You can export your data at any time:

- **Workout History**: Complete workout logs in CSV format
- **Exercise Library**: Your custom exercises and routines
- **Profile Data**: Personal information and settings
- **Analytics Data**: Your performance metrics and trends

To export your data:
1. Go to Settings → Export Data
2. Choose data types to export
3. Receive download link via email

### 3. Account Deletion

To permanently delete your account:

1. Go to Settings → Delete Account
2. Confirm your decision (this cannot be undone)
3. All data will be deleted within 30 days
4. Some data may be retained for legal requirements

### 4. Opt-Out Choices

You can opt out of:

- **Data Collection**: Disable certain features in settings
- **Analytics**: Anonymous usage tracking
- **Marketing Communications**: Unsubscribe from emails
- **Push Notifications**: Disable in device settings

## Children's Privacy

Titan SMRT is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@titanapp.com.

## Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active:

- **Workout Data**: Indefinitely (or until you delete it)

### 2. Deleted Accounts

When you delete your account:

- **Personal Data**: Permanently deleted within 30 days

### 3. Inactive Accounts

Accounts inactive for more than 24 months may be deleted. You will receive notification before deletion.
